## **Discussion/Study 2 - Genuine in Endurance, James 1:2-26.**

**Key Phrase:** *"… you know that the 'testing' of your faith produces endurance. And let endurance have its full effect, so that you may be mature and complete, lacking nothing."* James 1:3-4

**Scenario:**

The day-to-day presses us continually from family-life to work-life. To meet this press and never be found lacking, there is a process through which we can grow and excel – it’s called testing that produces endurance. But our confidence is that God is with us through every step of this process.

**Word Studies:**
1. Read James 1:2-26, [James 1:2-26 CSB - Trials and Maturity - Consider it a - Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James%201%3A2-26&version=CSB).
   1. Testing Gk. [Strong's Greek: 1383. δοκίμιον (dokimion) -- a testing (biblehub.com)](https://biblehub.com/greek/1383.htm).
      1. Scroll to/read *HELPS Word-studies and Thayer's Greek Lexicon* #1 & #2. What do you see as the relationship between "testing" and faith?
   2. Endurance Gk. {Strong's Greek: 5281. ὑπομονή (hupomoné) -- a remaining behind, a patient enduring (biblehub.com)](https://biblehub.com/greek/5281.htm).
      1. Scroll to/read the left column thru *Thayer's Greek Lexicon* #1 definition. In what ways do you see "endurance" as different from "patience"?
      2. Scroll further, what does *Strong's Exhaustive Concordance* add to its definition of endurance? How does this further impact your understanding of the word endurance.
