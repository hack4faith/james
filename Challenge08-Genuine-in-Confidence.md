## **Discussion/Study 8 - Genuine in Confidence, James 4:13 – 5:12.**


**Key Phrase:** *"…You are a mist that appears for a little while and then vanishes. Instead, you ought to say, ‘If it is the Lord’s will, we will live and do this or that.'"* James 4:14-15

**Scenario:**

The future – that which is always before us. It holds the probability for times of great sorrow and great joy, times of lack and abundance, times of suppression and conquest. How can we be confident in facing a future of uncertainty? Should we make plans or should we not? What’s our posture to be in relationship with others and God?
