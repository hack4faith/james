Discussion/Study 4 - Genuine in Actions, James 2:14-26.

Key Phrase: "…you see that faith was active together with his works, and by works, faith was made complete…" James 2:22

Scenario:

There are many cliches that we hear growing up from parents, teachers, coaches, etc. like, “walk the talk,” “practice what you preach,” and “actions speak louder than words.” The guidance here is that what we do, that is, what others see, is fully aligned with the words they hear us saying. A correlation to this wise guidance from a spiritual perspective is “faith without works (actions) is dead” which James teaches. As believers, how do we show the genuineness of our faith through our actions?

Making it Real – Faith with Shoes On

  1. In light of all the scriptures above, think through two metaphors:

      1. Think about the role of currency across the globe. Think about the role of currency in an individual’s life.
        1. What’s the importance of currency in any socio-economic structure?
        2. If you could look into someone’s investments, what might you learn about them?

    2. Think about Faith as the “boat/ship/kayak/water-skipper” and the “wake” that it leaves behind. What does the wake reveal about the floating object?
