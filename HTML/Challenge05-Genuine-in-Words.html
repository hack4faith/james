Discussion/Study 5 - Genuine in Words, James 3:1-12.

Key Phrase: "Blessing and cursing come out of the same mouth. My brothers and sisters, these things should not be this way." James 3:10

Scenario:

William Barclay writes, “Many a man speaks with perfect courtesy to strangers and even preaches love and gentleness, and yet snaps with impatient irritability at his own family. It has not been unknown for a man to speak with piety on Sunday and to curse a squad of workmen on Monday. … It has not been unknown for a woman to speak with sweet graciousness at a religious meeting and then to go outside to murder someone’s reputation with a malicious tongue.” (The Daily Study Bible Series, the Letters of James and Peter, Revised Edition 1976) Taming the tongue to be disciplined and genuine in all that it says is one of our greatest challenges as a believer.
