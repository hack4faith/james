Discussion/Study 3 - Genuine in Impartiality, James 2:1-13.

Key Phrase: "My brothers and sisters, do not show favoritism as you hold on to the faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ." James 2:1

Scenario:

We live in a day of tremendous polarization, whether political, racial, or socio-economic. There is a swirl of toxicity around us, but we of faith in the “glorious Lord Jesus Christ” are called to be and remain genuine and impartial in all our interactions. How do we rise to this level of genuineness and integrity?

The Problem and the Remedy for Impartiality:

  1. The problem of impartiality in believers.
    1. Read James 2:1-7, James 2:1-7 CSB - The Sin of Favoritism - My brothers and - Bible Gateway.
    2. How do you frame the problem? Is favoritism shown in other ways than just financial status?
  2. The remedy for impartiality in believers.
    1. Read James 2:8-13, James 2:8-13 CSB - Indeed, if you fulfill the royal law - Bible Gateway.
    2. What do you see as the remedy for showing favoritism?

Digging in - Building a Scriptural Perspective on Showing Genuine Impartiality:

  1. The following scriptures are not exhaustive examples but help establish our foundation. You’ll look these up in your Bible (or online) so you can see the context.
  2. The Old Testament:
    1. God’s promise to Abraham for all nations – Genesis 22:15-18
    2. The Hebrew law – Leviticus 19:15
    3. King Solomon’s writing – Proverbs 22:2
    4. A prophet’s writing – Malachi 2:8-10
  3. Jesus’ Life & Teaching
    1. Matthew 5:1-10
    2. Matthew 11:4-6
    3. Matthew 22:15-17
    4. Luke 4:16-19
  4. Peter, Paul, and John
    1. Acts 10:9-15
    2. Galatians 3:27-29
    3. 1 John 3:13-18
  5. From the above scriptures, what inferences can you make about the Biblical/scriptural perspective on impartiality?
  6. What practical ways can we align our mind and heart with these examples and principles?
