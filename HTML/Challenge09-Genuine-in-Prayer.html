Discussion/Study 9 - Genuine in Prayer, James 5:13-20.

Key Phrase: "…The prayer of a righteous person is powerful and effective." James 5:16 NIV

Scenario:

Prayer, an on-going, bi-directional, conversation with the ever-present God. What insights can we learn from James about this on-going conversation? What compels us to practice this bi-directional conversation with the ever-present God?

Being Genuine in Prayer:

  1. Read James 5:13-20, James 5:13-20 AMP - Is anyone among you suffering? He must - Bible Gateway.

Digging in:

  1. Verses 5:13-18:
    1. When should you pray?
    2. Why should you pray?
    3. How should you pray?
    4. Who should pray?
    5. What can be the results of prayer?
    6. Why might we not pray?
  2. Optional reading – the account of Elijah and the rain – 1 Kings 17 & 18: 1 Kings 17-18 NIV - Elijah Announces a Great Drought - Now - Bible Gateway.
  3. Looking back at other “tips” James provides us about prayer:
    1. James 1:5-8, What is/are James’ “tip(s)” for prayer here?
    2. James 4:2-3, What is/are James’ “tip(s)” for prayer here?
    3. James 4:7-10, What is/are James’ “tip(s)” for prayer here?
  4. Highlighting a couple of Jesus’ teachings on prayer:
    1. The Lord’s Prayer: Matthew 6:5-15 NIV - Prayer - “And when you pray, do not - Bible Gateway.
      1. What are your bullet list takeaways from this passage?
    2. Ask, Seek, Knock… how much more…: Matthew 7:7-12 NIV - Ask, Seek, Knock - “Ask and it will - Bible Gateway.
      1. What are your bullet list takeaways from this passage?
  5. Verses 5:19-20:
    1. What is James’ closing call to us as believers?
