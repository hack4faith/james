Discussion/Study 7 - Genuine in Relationships, James 4:1-12.

Key Phrase: "Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will lift you up." James 4:10

Scenario:

Someone once quipped, “If I didn’t have to deal with people life would be great!” Well, life is made up of dealing with others – and some of the most trying are those closest to us. But not only do we have the “horizontal” relationship with people, we have a “vertical” relationship with God to be considered. The focus on one axis over the other has a direct impact on the other – which relationship we prioritize is our moment-by-moment challenge.
