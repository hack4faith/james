## **Discussion/Study 3 - Genuine in Impartiality, James 2:1-13.**


**Key Phrase:** *"My brothers and sisters, do not show favoritism as you hold on to the faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ."* James 2:1

**Scenario:**

We live in a day of tremendous polarization, whether political, racial, or socio-economic. There is a swirl of toxicity around us, but we of faith in the “glorious Lord Jesus Christ” are called to be and remain genuine and impartial in all our interactions. How do we rise to this level of genuineness and integrity? 

**The Problem and the Remedy for Impartiality:**
1. The problem of impartiality in believers.
   1. Read James 2:1-7, [James 2:1-7 CSB - The Sin of Favoritism - My brothers and - Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James%202%3A1-7&version=CSB).
   2. How do you frame the problem? Is favoritism shown in other ways than just financial status?
2. The remedy for impartiality in believers.
   1. Read James 2:8-13, [James 2:8-13 CSB - Indeed, if you fulfill the royal law - Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James%202%3A8-13&version=CSB).
   2. What do you see as the remedy for showing favoritism?
